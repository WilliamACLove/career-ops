**Red flags:**

> ⚠️ **Bar admission / jurisdiction signal:** Acme Corp is headquartered in California and the JD states the role "supports our California legal team." The candidate holds a New York bar admission and is not yet California-admitted. California's Registered In-House Counsel program allows an out-of-state-licensed attorney to register with the State Bar without sitting the California bar exam, provided they work exclusively for a single qualifying employer -- this is a moderate, not a hard, gate (unlike a law-firm lateral posting, where jurisdiction mismatch is usually close to disqualifying). Registration typically takes 4-8 weeks. Confirm with Acme's legal recruiting contact whether registration can proceed post-offer, and whether any interim state-specific admission requirement applies to the role's day-to-day work.

This signal does not change the High Confidence tier above -- it is a candidate-side eligibility note, not a posting-legitimacy concern.
