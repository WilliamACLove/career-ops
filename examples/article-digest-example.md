## $2.1B Cross-Border Acquisition -- Data Infrastructure

**Hero metrics:** $2.1B deal value, 6 specialist workstreams coordinated, 4-month signing-to-close timeline

**Matter summary:** Represented the acquirer (a publicly traded enterprise software company) in the cross-border acquisition of a European data-infrastructure company. Drafted the merger agreement and disclosure schedules and coordinated due diligence across IP, privacy, employment, tax, antitrust, and real estate workstreams.

**Key decisions:**
- Structured the deal as a two-step merger to preserve target-side tax attributes across jurisdictions
- Negotiated a customized IP assignment schedule to address open-source contamination risk flagged during diligence
- Built a cross-border disclosure-schedule template later reused on two subsequent deals

**Proof points:**
- Closed on schedule with no post-signing indemnification claims in the first 18 months
- Due diligence findings prevented a $4M post-closing dispute over IP ownership
- Deal referenced (generically, no client name) in a firm CLE presentation on cross-border tech M&A

## $340M Series D Financing -- AI-Infrastructure Company

**Hero metrics:** $340M raised, investor-side technology counsel, 6-week negotiation window

**Matter summary:** Advised the lead investor on the technology and IP diligence workstream of a Series D financing for an AI-infrastructure company. Negotiated investor-side technology representations, IP warranties, and a data-rights side letter.

**Key decisions:**
- Pushed for a narrower IP representation carve-out after diligence surfaced a third-party licensing dependency
- Negotiated a data-rights side letter addressing model-training data provenance, ahead of standard market terms at the time
- Coordinated with outside IP counsel to confirm freedom-to-operate on the company's core inference pipeline

**Proof points:**
- Closed within the client's target timeline despite a late-stage IP diligence finding
- Data-rights side letter language was adopted as the client's template for two subsequent portfolio investments

## SaaS / MSA / DPA Negotiation Program -- Enterprise Software & Fintech Clients

**Hero metrics:** 150+ agreements negotiated, average cycle time cut from 21 to 9 business days, clause-library playbook adopted firmwide

**Matter summary:** Built and ran a high-volume commercial contracting practice for enterprise software and fintech clients -- SaaS agreements, MSAs, and DPAs -- standardizing fallback positions for the recurring negotiation points (liability caps, data residency, security addenda, audit rights).

**Key decisions:**
- Built a clause-library playbook mapping each recurring negotiation point to a primary position, a fallback, and a walk-away
- Introduced a DPA rider addressing state privacy-law patchwork (CCPA/CPRA plus emerging state analogs) ahead of most peer playbooks
- Trained two junior associates on the playbook, reducing partner review time on routine agreements

**Proof points:**
- Average negotiation cycle time cut from 21 to 9 business days after playbook adoption
- Playbook adopted firmwide across the technology transactions group
- Zero escalations to the general counsel level across the tracked matter volume
