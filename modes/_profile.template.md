## Your Comp Targets

<!-- Research comp ranges for YOUR target roles. Directional defaults below,
     as of 2026-07 — REPLACE with your own research. -->

**Law firms (Cravath/market scale — in flux H2 2026):** 2024-scale bases Y1 $225K → Y8 $435K; on June 2, 2026 Milbank reset the scale (effective 7/1/2026) to roughly Y1 $235K → Y8 $455K, matched by several firms but not yet universal — carry both scales with an as-of date. Year-end bonuses (2025 scale) $20K (Y1) → $115K (Y7+), typically gated at 1,900-2,000 billable hours. Midsize/regional firms: expect 60-80% of Cravath base with smaller, discretionary bonuses.

**In-house:** expect a base cut of 20-50% vs BigLaw all-in, offset by bonus target + equity + hours. Rough national cash bands: Counsel $107K-$179K base; Senior Counsel $157K-$220K; AGC ~$266K-$330K; DGC $300K-$450K cash. Bonus targets by level: Counsel 15-25% of base; AGC 30-45%; DGC 50-75%; GC 75-100%. At tech companies, annual equity often equals or exceeds bonus. Tech/coastal runs 20-40% higher.

**Government:** GS scale (2026: GS-11 $63,795 → GS-15 up to $164,301 base) plus locality (~15-45%); AUSA AD plan capped ~$195K; financial regulators (SEC/FDIC/Fed/OCC/CFPB) pay 40-80% above GS. Loan repayment (ASLRP) and PSLF are comp line-items.

**Sources:** Cravath scale + Above the Law bonus tracker + Biglaw Investor (firms); NALP surveys (midsize/regional); BarkerGilmore + MLA comp surveys, L Suite (tech GC), ACC benchmarking (in-house); OPM GS tables + agency pay plans (government); Brightflag/CLOC (legal ops).

## Your Negotiation Scripts

<!-- Adapt to YOUR situation, market, and track. Legal negotiation is
     track-specific: know which game you're playing before you speak. -->

**BigLaw associate (lockstep — base is NOT negotiable):**
> "I understand base is scale. I'd like to discuss class-year placement — my experience maps to [year] work — along with start date and the clerkship bonus."

*(Negotiable: class-year placement ±1, start date, clerkship bonus per published scale, modest relocation. Don't negotiate base — it signals you don't know the market.)*

**In-house (negotiate LEVEL first, then the package):**
> "Based on the scope described, this maps to a Senior Counsel level. I'm targeting [RANGE from profile.yml] base with a [X]% bonus target, plus the equity grant and refresh schedule. I'm flexible on structure — what matters is total package and level."

**In-house sign-on (buying out a forfeited firm bonus):**
> "I'm walking away from a $75K bonus paid in January. A sign-on that bridges that gap makes the timing work for both of us."

**Government (essentially non-negotiable):**
> "I'd like to request step-setting based on superior qualifications — my [X years of directly relevant experience / prior salary] supports placement above step 1 within the grade."

## Your Jurisdiction & Location Policy

<!-- Adapt to YOUR admissions. Bar admissions are the PRIMARY geographic
     constraint for attorneys — list them in config/profile.yml under legal: -->

**Bar admissions drive geography:**
- Your admitted jurisdictions (from `legal.bar_admissions` in profile.yml) are your default market.
- UBE portability: score transfer available in 42 jurisdictions within typical 2-4 year windows. California and Florida are portability walls (FL has no reciprocity at all).
- Admission on motion: ~40 jurisdictions, usually 3-of-5 or 5-of-7 years of active practice required.
- In-house exception: registered in-house counsel status (ABA Model Rule 5.5(d)) lets you work for a single employer in most states without full local admission.

**In forms:**
- Follow your actual availability and admissions from profile.yml
- State jurisdiction + year of admission up front; note pending transfers or registrations in free-text fields

**In evaluations (scoring):**
- Jurisdiction mismatch: **law firms → heavy penalty** (hard gate unless a realistic transfer/motion path exists); **in-house → light penalty** (registration usually available); **federal government / legal tech → no penalty** (any active license suffices)
- Remote dimension for hybrid outside your metro: score **3.0** (not 1.0)
- Only score 1.0 if JD says "must be in-office 4-5 days/week, no exceptions" (note: litigation and firm roles trend office-heavy — treat stated hybrid norms as real)
