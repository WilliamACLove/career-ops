Portfolio work for an attorney means: writing samples, published articles or client alerts, CLE talks, bar association leadership, a public playbook or contract-template resource, or — for the Legal Tech / Legal Engineer archetype — an actual legal-tech side project (a clause library, an intake automation, an AI-review workflow). Evaluate any of these with the matrix below.

Scoring matrix with 6 dimensions (1–5):

| Dimension | Weight | 5 = ... | 1 = ... |
|-----------|------|---------|---------|
| Signal for target roles | 25% | Directly demonstrates a JD skill (e.g. a SaaS negotiation playbook for a Commercial Counsel target) | Not related to any target practice area |
| Uniqueness | 20% | Nobody has published this angle | Very common (another generic "GDPR basics" explainer) |
| Demo ability | 20% | Shareable in 2 min (published piece, live tool, one-page playbook excerpt) | Confidential or unshareable work only |
| Metrics potential | 15% | Clear metrics (contract turnaround time cut, matters closed per quarter, playbook adoption rate, motion win rate, CLE talks delivered / publications placed) | No metrics possible |
| Time to MVP | 10% | 1 week | 3+ months |
| STAR story potential | 10% | Rich story with judgment calls and trade-offs | Rote execution only |

**Confidentiality gate (before anything else):** client work is presumptively confidential. A writing sample must be the candidate's own work, minimally edited by others, and either a public filing or scrubbed with client consent. Never suggest publishing anything that identifies a client or matter that isn't already public.

## "Interview Pack" Requirements

For each approved project:
1. **One-pager**: what it is + who it serves + metrics + how it maps to the target archetype
2. **Demo**: published link, polished writing sample, or 2 min walkthrough (for legal-tech projects: live URL or recording)
3. **Postmortem**: what worked, what didn't, what you'd counsel differently next time

## 80/20 Plan

- Week 1 → MVP with core metric (draft the article, scrub the sample, build the working playbook excerpt)
- Week 2 → polish + interview pack

## Verdicts

- **BUILD** → plan with weekly milestones
- **SKIP** → why and what to do instead
- **PIVOT TO [alternative]** → more impactful variant (e.g. the same expertise as a bar-association CLE talk instead of an unread blog post)
